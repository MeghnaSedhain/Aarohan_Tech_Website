# Team Member Photos Directory

You can add real photos for the Aarohan Technology team members directly into this folder:

### Recommended File Names:
- `ceo.jpg` — Chief Executive Officer (CEO)
- `cto.jpg` — Chief Technology Officer (CTO / ATO)
- `hr.jpg` — Head of Human Resources (HR)
- `engineer1.jpg` — IT Engineer 1 (Lead Full Stack)
- `engineer2.jpg` — IT Engineer 2 (Senior AI & ML)
- `engineer3.jpg` — IT Engineer 3 (Cloud & DevOps)
- `engineer4.jpg` — IT Engineer 4 (Frontend & Mobile)
- `engineer5.jpg` — IT Engineer 5 (Backend & Systems)

### How to Apply:
1. Copy your photos into this folder (e.g. `client/public/team/ceo.jpg`).
2. On the website (at `http://localhost:5173#team`), hover over any team card and click **"Edit Info"**.
3. In the "Photo URL" field, enter `/team/ceo.jpg` (or any image URL) and click **"Save to MongoDB"**!
