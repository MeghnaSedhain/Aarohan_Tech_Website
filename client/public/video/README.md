# Aarohan Technology - Video Assets Directory

This folder holds the video files displayed in the Aarohan Technology Hero showcase.

## Active Files
- `company-reel.webm`: The primary default showcase reel for the company.
- `ai-reel.webm`: AI-powered video creation showcase.
- `ai-creative-reel.webm`: Digital innovation and creative tech clip.

## How to add your own company video:
1. Copy your video file (MP4 or WebM format) into this folder (`client/public/video/`).
2. Rename it to `company-reel.mp4` or `company-reel.webm`.
3. It will immediately play in the website's Hero section!

You can also paste any custom video URL (MP4 or YouTube embed) directly through the **"Change Video"** button on the website.
